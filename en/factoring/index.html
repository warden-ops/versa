The product

Factoring

Versa Capital presents factoring as a solution for liquidity. It is used to increase the business’s liquidity, to pay suppliers sooner and to obtain discounts.

It also removes the administrative formalities tied to managing receivables. Accounts receivable are handled by a specialized team. That is the published mechanism: receivables and accounts receivable are at the centre of the product, not the purchase of a piece of equipment.

The product is used by businesses of every size. It is particularly suited to businesses that are starting up, growing or restructuring. A business with seasonal or periodic sales can use it to support continued growth and stability.

Versa Capital states that factoring is not subject to spending restrictions, unlike commercial loans. That is the published distinction. It does not describe a rate, an advance or an approval time: those figures are not on the original page.

What is published

  • Increase liquidity
  • Pay suppliers sooner and obtain discounts
  • Receivables managed by a specialized team

In detail

What the file covers

The term loan, also offered, finances an equipment purchase and spreads payments over several years, with terms and due dates set in advance. Leasing is a contract with a lessor. Factoring stays centred on receivables. The choice depends on the need: operating liquidity, or financing an asset.

Versa Capital prepares the file and presents it to its financial partners. The business has a single point of contact to compare the options. The site states more than 20 financial partners. The original About page showed 25 financial partners and 15 dealer partners: both sets of figures are published, and the firm can confirm the current count.

A request is made by phone at 1-833-508-3772, by email at info@versacapital.ca, or with the form. An advisor calls back within 24 hours to answer questions and review the needs.

Personal information is disclosed only within Canada, unless an exception requires consent. The hosting stated by Versa Capital is in Quebec. The published person for an access, correction or deletion request is Frédéric Parent, at fparent@versacapital.ca.

The accounting and tax treatment of a contract depends on its clauses and on the business’s situation. Versa Capital does not give tax advice. An accountant or a tax advisor must confirm the treatment before signing.

Seasonal or periodic sales create gaps between the sale and the collection. Factoring is presented as a way through those gaps, because it is not subject to the spending restrictions of a commercial loan. The file stays supported: understand the business, structure the request, position it, compare the options, then follow through to funding.
