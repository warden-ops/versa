The team

Integrity, simplicity and commitment

Our only objective is to combine our team’s expertise with our clients’ requirements in order to provide financing solutions that are flexible, competitive and tailored.

We guarantee you the best service.

Use our team’s expertise and advice to plan financing that is sound, solid and realistic. Versa Capital can offer a range of capital solutions: equipment financing, a conditional sale, refinancing and factoring.

The client experience is irreproachable. Integrity is one of the values. The team answers questions and guides the client step by step through the financing process.

Versa Capital offers access to several financing solutions, favourable terms and tailored plans.

Versa Capital owns and partners with equipment dealerships, and has access to favourable terms through its financial partners. A line of credit is also available: contact the team.
