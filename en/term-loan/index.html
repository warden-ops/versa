The product

Term loan

The term loan is used to carry out an equipment purchase while keeping liquidity for day-to-day operations. Versa Capital presents it as support for the growth of the business. It can finance a wide variety of assets.

Payments are spread over several years. The business therefore keeps more liquidity than if it paid for the asset in cash. The payment terms and the due dates are set in advance: the schedule is known at signing.

The loan has two effects on management. The loan improves management of the capital budget, because the purchase is planned over time. It is also presented as a way to strengthen the structure of the balance sheet.

Leasing, offered alongside, is a contract with a lessor. In a capital lease, the contract is structured to allow ownership at the end of the term. In an operating lease, with no purchase guarantee, the business has no commitment to buy the asset. The term loan for a purchase is different: the business is the buyer and repays a loan.

In short

  • Payments spread over several years
  • Terms and due dates set in advance
  • Capital budget and balance sheet structure

In detail

What the file covers

The choice between the loan and leasing depends on the project, the profile, liquidity, how long the asset will be held and the objectives. Versa Capital does not give tax advice. The accounting and tax treatment depends on the clauses and on the business’s situation. An accountant or a tax advisor must confirm it before signing.

Equipment refinancing, which is separate, uses an asset already paid for to replenish working capital. A working capital loan finances operations, not the purchase of an asset. The term loan remains the purchase product. Versa Capital compares these options rather than imposing one.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the term loan?

It is a loan to buy equipment while keeping liquidity for day-to-day operations. It finances a wide variety of assets.

How are the payments organized?

Payments are spread over several years. The terms and the due dates are set in advance.

What is the effect on the budget and the balance sheet?

The loan improves management of the capital budget. It is also presented as a way to strengthen the structure of the balance sheet.

How does it differ from leasing?

With a term loan to purchase, the business buys and repays a loan. Leasing is a contract with a lessor, as a capital lease or as an operating lease with no purchase guarantee.

What assets can be financed?

A wide variety of assets. Versa Capital already names construction, transportation, forestry, agricultural, industrial, medical and office equipment, and other categories on its equipment page.

How do I apply?

By phone at 1-833-508-3772, by email at info@versacapital.ca, or with the form. A Versa Capital advisor will reply.

What documents should you prepare for a financing request?

The form asks for your name, company, email, phone, the type of financing and a short description of the project. Versa Capital then prepares the file and presents it to its financial partners. Any further documents depend on the product and the partner. They are requested when the file is reviewed, not listed in advance on this page.

Do you finance used equipment?

Yes. Equipment financing covers new or used equipment. The file follows the project, the business profile and how long you expect to keep the asset. The terms are in the partner’s offer, after the file is reviewed.
