The request

What the request contains

The request includes the full name, the company, the phone number, the email, the financing amount and the service requested, then a space for comments.

The amounts offered are: $1,000 to $25,000, $25,001 to $50,000, $50,001 to $100,000, $100,001 to $250,000, and $250,001 and over.

The request can cover leasing, factoring, equipment refinancing, a term loan and the other financing products.

You can also call 1-833-508-3772 or write to info@versacapital.ca. A Versa Capital advisor will reply.

The request is addressed to info@versacapital.ca.

FAQ

Common questions

What documents should you prepare for a financing request?

The form asks for your name, company, email, phone, the type of financing and a short description of the project. Versa Capital then prepares the file and presents it to its financial partners. Any further documents depend on the product and the partner. They are requested when the file is reviewed, not listed in advance on this page.
