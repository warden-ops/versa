Privacy policy

Versa Capital’s privacy policy and SMS service policy.

This page sets out how Versa Capital collects, uses and protects personal information, including for the SMS service.

Our guidelines for protecting your personal information.

  • Put sound management and protection practices in place to keep your personal information secure and to govern its use.
  • Respect your rights regarding the personal information we hold about you. You may exercise them at any time.
  • Be transparent. We give you the relevant information about our personal information practices.
  • Act responsibly. Our employees and partners are aware of our personal information practices.

Only the personal information that is needed is collected.

  • We request the information from the signing client.
  • Depending on the circumstances, the client may authorize a third person to send personal information.
  • Personal information may be collected by email, by phone, through our paper and online forms, or through cookies when you visit our website.

What may be requested:

Identification information such as name, date of birth, mailing address, email, telephone number, identity documents, and social insurance number.

Financial information such as the balance sheet, bank statements and the credit report.

For what purposes

  • To know you, to know who you are and to confirm your identity.
  • To contact you in order to answer questions, understand your needs and review your requests for products and services.
  • To produce contracts, process payments and transfer information to the lenders with whom we partner.
  • To comply with the law and manage risks, such as preventing and containing fraud or unauthorized activity.
  • To meet our legal obligations and the requirements of the various authorities.

To whom may we disclose your personal information?

  • Our commercial equipment financing brokers.
  • Our sales coordinators.
  • Other companies, such as the financial institutions that take on your types of financing.
  • Credit reporting agencies such as Equifax.
  • Insurance companies, to obtain confirmation of insurance on your equipment or for life and disability insurance.
  • Courts, regulatory authorities or self-regulatory organizations.
  • Fraud-management bodies such as police services.

Information is disclosed only within Canada unless an exception requires consent.

Obtaining your consent

We obtain your consent directly from you before collecting your personal information. It may be collected by our brokers or by members of our sales coordination team. Your consent is requested again if we wish to use or disclose your information for a purpose other than the one for which it was collected.

In some cases, the law allows personal information to be collected, used or disclosed without your consent. For example, to carry out statistical studies, to provide a requested product or service, to take necessary steps in a case of fraud, or if the law requires disclosure. (Court)

We take meaningful steps to protect your personal information

Every member of our team helps protect your information. Our internal procedures set out each person’s responsibilities in this matter.

  • Access to information is limited to the people who need it to carry out their duties.
  • Employees are trained to handle information with care.
  • We review employee access on an ongoing basis according to their responsibilities.
  • Security measures are in place to protect our platforms and computer systems. (Multi-factor authentication, encrypted data, monitoring of the facilities)
  • We will never ask for your password or your personal identification number.
  • We will not contact you for the sole purpose of obtaining your personal information.

Information is kept for a limited time

Information is kept only for as long as needed to meet the purpose for which it was requested, or to comply with our legal obligations. A schedule and rules are in place to guide employees on when information should be destroyed.

Your rights in your information are respected

Consent to the use of your personal data may be withdrawn at any time. We will then no longer be able to provide the service for which the information was collected. At the client’s request, we will provide, within a reasonable time, all the information we hold in the file. Any person may therefore know the information we hold, consult it, correct it and ask us to delete it. These requests must be made in writing to the person responsible for the protection of personal information.

How to reach us

To request access, correction or deletion, to file a complaint about the handling of personal information, or to ask for help following up on an incident, contact the person responsible and provide the necessary information at the addresses below.

1. Purpose and acceptance

By requesting, accepting, using or replying to the text messaging service of Versa Capital Inc. (“Versa Capital”, “we”, “our”), you accept these terms. The service includes SMS, MMS and any successor mobile messaging technology.

The exchanges are meant to support individual communications related to a request, a file, a service follow-up or an existing business relationship. Consent to receive SMS is not a mandatory condition for obtaining our services when another reasonable means of communication is available.

2. Consent and electronic record

When you provide your mobile number, start an SMS conversation, reply to a message or otherwise consent to the exchange, you authorize Versa Capital to send you messages related to that conversation. You accept that an electronic record may document your consent, your requests and your communication choices.

You must be the holder of the number provided or be authorized to use it. You are responsible for telling us if the number changes, is transferred or no longer belongs to you.

3. Frequency and content

Frequency varies with your exchanges with a Versa Capital employee and with the requests you make. An initial message may introduce the service, identify the sender and recall how to get help or unsubscribe.

Messages may cover file coordination, a document request, a service question, an appointment or a reply to your request. Do not send passwords, social insurance numbers, full banking information or other highly sensitive information by SMS.

4. Charges and availability

Versa Capital does not charge a fee to send or receive messages in this service. Message, data, roaming or other charges may still be billed by your wireless provider. Delivery depends on networks and providers and cannot be guaranteed.

5. Unsubscribe

You can stop the SMS in a conversation by replying STOP. The keywords QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE may also be recognized. A confirmation of the unsubscribe may be sent.

The unsubscribe applies to that conversation and to the employee’s Teams number. It does not block necessary email or phone communications when they are allowed, nor messages required by law. To start SMS again, you must give consent again.

6. SMS service privacy

We use the information provided in the SMS service to deliver messages, reply to you, document the exchanges, follow your file, provide support and meet our legal and security obligations.

Information may be processed by Microsoft, platform providers, telecommunications companies and other providers that help route, store, secure or support the messages. Those parties may use the information only to provide their services or to meet their legal obligations.

Mobile information, including the telephone number, subscription data and consent to text messaging, is not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotion.

We may disclose information when the law, an order, a regulatory inquiry or a valid government request requires it, or when it is reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, protect security, defend our rights or limit our liability.

7. Retention and security

SMS messages and their metadata may be kept in the systems of Versa Capital and of its providers according to the applicable retention rules, the needs of the file and legal requirements. Versa Capital applies reasonable administrative, technical and physical measures, but no transmission over a mobile network is entirely free of risk.

If an exchange must contain sensitive information or confidential documents, an employee may ask you to use a safer channel, such as encrypted email, a portal or an approved document space.

8. User responsibilities

You agree to use the service lawfully and respectfully, not to transmit malicious, fraudulent or unlawful content or content that infringes the rights of others, and not to try to bypass security measures. Versa Capital may suspend the SMS exchange in case of abuse, a security risk or a legal obligation.
